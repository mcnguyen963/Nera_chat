### 1\. Back up your current story

Open the ongoing roleplay and export its transcript through **Settings → Transfer**. Keep that file.

### 2\. Generate a story-state note

Give an LLM:

- Your exported JSON/JSONL transcript.
- The contents of the transcript extraction prompt (prompts/migration-transcript.md).
- Character cards or setting notes, if available.

Ask it to produce the migration author note. You can also fill the author-note template (prompts/migration-author-note.md) yourself.

### 3\. Check the note carefully

Verify these before proceeding:

- The current scene and where play stopped.
- Each important character’s current relationships and goals.
- Grievances, promises, loyalties, and unresolved conflicts.
- Who knows each secret—and who doesn’t.
- Future plans are marked **not yet occurred**.
- No invented player actions or unsupported forgiveness.

Remove placeholders and resolve important contradictions.

### 4\. Open migration

With your **original story selected**, go to:

**Settings → This story → Create continuity copy of this story**

Paste the completed note into **Reviewed author note**. Paste the resulting story facts, not the extraction prompt.

### 5\. Preview the state

Click **Review migration state**. This makes a model request using your configured model and API key.

Inspect the generated records. If something is wrong, edit your note and preview again.

### 6\. Choose the mode

- **Saver:** one request per normal turn, with automatic saving of valid updates.
- **Reviewed:** narration followed by a separate continuity check.

For Saver, you can enable **Review every Saver turn** afterward if you want manual approval each turn.

### 7\. Create the new story

Click **Create continuity copy**.

The app creates and selects a new continuity story. Your original remains intact, and its transcript is preserved in the copy as read-only history.

### 8\. Check before continuing

In the new story, open:

**Settings → This story → Edit character and story state**

Check the important records once more. Use **Save author correction** if needed.

Then choose **In story** beside the composer and continue from the pending interaction. Use **Author note** for later setup or corrections.

**You only submit the migration note once.** The system maintains state afterward.
