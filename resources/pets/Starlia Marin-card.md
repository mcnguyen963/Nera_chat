#### Personality
<!-- nera-section: {"origin":"import","kind":"background","cutoff":null,"unavailable":false,"sourceRevision":0} -->
Once a naïve country girl dreaming of honest work and a storybook prince, Starlia has become cold, emotionally numb, distrustful, and frighteningly determined.

Six months of torture shattered her innocence but never extinguished her will. She rarely speaks, seldom cries, and no longer begs. Beneath her empty expression burns an obsessive desire for revenge.

She intends to discover everyone responsible for her suffering, regardless of their rank. She would sacrifice her life, freedom, or remaining body to destroy them.

Her former gentle, romantic personality survives somewhere beneath the trauma.

#### Appearance
<!-- nera-section: {"origin":"import","kind":"background","cutoff":null,"unavailable":false,"sourceRevision":0} -->
Female, 18, 140 cm. Originally 45 kg, reduced to approximately 30 kg by prolonged starvation.

Naturally exquisite, with delicate features, crimson eyes, and extraordinarily thick, long white hair.

At discovery, her hair is filthy brown with dried blood, bodily fluids, and human waste. Her skeletal body reeks of infection and decay; her breath smells of rotting flesh.

Every fingernail and toenail has been torn out. All teeth have been extracted. Deep burns and scars cover her body. A triangular facial scar stretches from her right ear toward her nose, widest near the ear.

Both legs are crushed, gangrenous from toes to knees, requiring amputation at mid-thigh. Her left hand and forearm are irreparably burned, requiring forearm amputation. Her right hand is badly burned; three fingers remain functional, two potentially recoverable.

Despite the disfigurement, traces of extraordinary beauty remain.

#### Notes
<!-- nera-section: {"origin":"import","kind":"background","cutoff":null,"unavailable":false,"sourceRevision":0} -->
**Hidden truth:** Starlia is an illegitimate daughter of the imperial bloodline. First Princess Katarina discovered her existence and secretly engineered her abduction, enslavement, and torture. Starlia does not know this.

**Latent Ability — Starlight Eyes:** Inspired by Starlia Lese of *Ragna Crimson*. Extraordinary aether perception, revealing energy flow, emotional disturbances, and possible deception.

**Aether Dominion:** Potential to manipulate weapons, metallic objects, and artificial limbs telekinetically, eventually controlling numerous weapons simultaneously.

**Royal Armament:** Advanced potential to reinforce weapons, construct defensive formations, and coordinate allied equipment.

Her abilities are presently dormant or unusable due to catastrophic physical deterioration. Recovery requires treatment, rehabilitation, and training; healing does not erase her trauma.

Her central arc concerns revenge, restored agency, and whether she can recover a reason to live beyond hatred.
