#### Personality
<!-- nera-section: {"origin":"user","kind":"canon","cutoff":null} -->
Ambitious, playful, stubborn and competitive. Takes promises, training, household loyalty and independence seriously; dislikes control and increasingly rejects becoming another Cassian. Confident enough to be arrogant, respects competence and direct challenge, conceals his real limits. Campaign atrocities, including his own choices, remain consequential rather than erased by affection or victory.

#### Appearance
<!-- nera-section: {"origin":"user","kind":"canon","cutoff":null} -->
Pale, apparently slender; straight black hair and black eyes. Fitted dark Veyrath clothes with silver thorn-and-tower fastenings conceal his extraordinary body.

#### Status
<!-- nera-section: {"origin":"user","kind":"canon","cutoff":null} -->
**Core.** M; 18; Crownspire Y1 Class A #2. **Arc 4 / P6; potential Arc 6, true ceiling unknown.** Cassian’s third recognized child; Ilyra’s son; hereditary commander of exactly 5,000 Blackwing fighters. Roughly two years of pre-Academy Eastern campaigning ended in victory and gave genuine lethal combat and operational experience. He is experienced, while older officers can exceed his breadth of judgment. As of 04/11: personal Sarthe & Vell deposits of **145,854g** are accessible on demand. **10,000g** is committed to Starlia’s limbs; **500g** opens first, then milestone releases require a surgeon, witnessed receipt and her fitting approval. The household safe contains **8g 3s 42c**.

#### Notes
<!-- nera-section: {"origin":"user","kind":"canon","cutoff":null} -->
Seven years of conditioning produced P6 force, speed, endurance and durability. Familiar spells cast effectively instantly. Precision/heavy aether beams, rapid barrages, numerous independent targets and vector redirection permit curved and multi-angle attacks. Ilyra-linked ancient glyph literacy permits recognition and use without universal mastery. **Heaven Splitter is fully mastered: unrestricted output literally cleaves mountains and splits the sky.** Academy demonstrations use tiny fractions; his stated limits can be deliberate understatement. Still Interval interrupts transitions through timing/contact, not completed soul bonds. Difficulty comes from judgment, politics, command, incomplete intelligence, protecting others and collateral damage; ordinary foes cannot suddenly equal his unrestricted power. His voluntary words and actions belong to the player.
