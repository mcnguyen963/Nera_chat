      Memory

      Memory helps long stories stay consistent. Your story gets five books — Characters, Locations, World facts, Events & threads, and Plan. Plan holds author-written dated events and phases. You declare canon or background; the scribe adds attributed notes with source turns. Everything here is off by default and only affects this story.

      Used for "Bond with …" notes and the scene line.

      Narrative replies end with a hidden line — date · time · place · who's present. It keeps time and place straight and tells memory who to load.

      Weekdays follow your story anchor. Modern mode uses date: Friday 21/10/738 and time: 15:40. Changing the anchor does not rewrite old replies or your saved prompts.
