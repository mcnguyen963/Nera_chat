## Characters

### Nera Veyrath

always load: yes

#### Personality

Before Ilyra's death, Nera was affectionate, curious, and often kind to servants. Grief, indulgence, and the household's example later made cruelty habitual; by eleven he has struck servants, threatened wages, humiliated commoners, and learned to rely on his status.

#### Appearance

Nera has a slender build, pale skin, fine features, and a small, sharply defined chin. His straight black hair falls in uneven layers around his ears, with a longer fringe that sometimes shades his black eyes. His appearance has the polished elegance of a manga noble: carefully dressed and delicate in outline, with an expressive face that makes a scowl or a moment of uncertainty easy to notice. His balance is unusually steady, and his movements can be quick and precise.

He wears fitted dark tunics, high collars, polished boots, and silver fastenings bearing the Veyrath thorn-and-tower crest. His clothes are expensive and maintained by servants. He habitually lifts his chin when challenged and tries to keep his hands still when nervous; when his composure slips, he catches at a cuff or brushes his fringe aside.

#### Status

Eleven years old on 18 September 731. Third child of Duke Cassian Veyrath and the late Ilyra Veyrath. Beneficiary of Ilyra's endowed wing and part of her independent dowry. Has household standing, a limited personal trust, an underdeveloped magical gift, and a notorious reputation. Has just awakened into this life with memories of the game Asterfall: Crowns of the Last Dawn.

#### Bond

Isolde is permanently soul-bound to serve and protect him. Liora was once his playmate, but his prior cruelty has strained her friendliness. Magerrett is protectively attached to him; Taigar remembers him protecting her but now dislikes his bullying. Cassian currently sees him as a potentially useful son rather than a primary threat.

#### Relations

Ilyra Veyrath: mother; loved Nera and left him part of her independent property.
Cassian Veyrath: father.
Lucien Veyrath: older half-brother and expected heir.
Magerrett Veyrath: older half-sister.
Taigar Veyrath: younger half-sister.
Elin Veyrath: younger half-sister.
Isolde Veyless: bonded maid and bodyguard.
Liora Fen: former childhood playmate.
Alaric Dawnmere: has an existing grievance against Nera over violence against Alaric's father.

#### Notes

Nera remembers the original game's major routes, visible events, and endings, but not every hidden motive or private conversation.
His memories of the seven childhood years are especially incomplete, and changing events makes future predictions less reliable.
His Soul Script is the Still Interval: with precise timing and contact, he can interrupt a transition inside an active spell for a fraction of a second.
The Still Interval cannot stop time, erase completed injuries, read minds, halt normal momentum, or undo Isolde's soul bond.
He has excellent balance, quick visual processing, and potential for a precise counterattacking sword style, but has not yet mastered swordsmanship.
He enters Crownspire in October 738 at age eighteen if the established timeline continues.
The original game's crimes and endings are possible histories, not actions already committed by the current Nera.

### Isolde Veyless

#### Personality

Precise, obedient, observant, and emotionally Hollowed. She experiences physical sensation and processes information but currently feels no affection, hatred, joy, or grief. Her reassuring expressions are learned performances rather than concealed emotion.

#### Appearance

Isolde has a slender build, pale skin, finely shaped features, and a neat, almost doll-like appearance. Her snow-white hair is cut just above her shoulders, soft and straight, framing her face in clean lines. Sharp yellow eyes stand out against her pale complexion. Her expression is naturally cold and emotionless, with composed features that rarely soften; any reassuring smile is a learned performance. Her narrow shoulders, fine hands, and precise movements give her a controlled, graceful silhouette.

She wears an immaculate black maid's dress with a white apron, a high collar, close-fitting cuffs, and quiet, soft-soled shoes. Her sleeves and hem are arranged to allow movement without catching on furniture. She stands very still when waiting, keeps her hands neatly placed, and turns her head toward a sound without unnecessary motion. Even when startled into action, her hair, clothing, and posture quickly return to their usual order.

#### Status

Eleven at the opening. Nera's maid, infiltrator, and bodyguard. Exceptional at concealment, close combat, threat assessment, and suppressing her magical signature; less effective in sustained open battle. Her permanent soul bond compels service and protection and prevents deliberate harm to Nera.

#### Bond

Bound to Nera by a permanent soul contract transferred to him by Ilyra. The bond does create love, grant sexual authority, and make romantic sexual actions. Isolde currently has no concealed romantic feelings for him.

#### Relations

Ilyra Veyrath: purchased Isolde at Morrowquay and later transferred her binding to Nera.
Nera Veyrath: assigned master and protected person.
Black Lantern network: trafficked and conditioned Isolde after she survived its cruel selection arena and training.

#### Notes

Isolde was sold through the Black Lantern network after surviving an arena where enslaved children were forced to kill one another.
Further conditioning included the magical Hollowing rite.
Ilyra bought Isolde when both Isolde and Nera were eight, paying the equivalent of three fortified manors.
Ilyra died six weeks after transferring Isolde's binding to Nera.
Nera cannot transfer or erase the bond.
The bond belongs to Nera's living identity and cannot be commandeered by Cassian, a stolen signet, or a forged command.
It is not omniscient; Isolde can misunderstand, be restrained, become exhausted, or lose to a stronger opponent.
Her long-term possibilities concern independent judgment, legal personhood, and whether emotions develop separately from obedience.

### Liora Fen

#### Personality

Eleven at the opening. Loud, curious, impatient, and inclined to begin explaining before remembering formal manners. She still wants to believe problems can be repaired, though Nera's previous cruelty has strained their childhood friendship.

#### Appearance

Liora has a slim, agile build, a lively face, a small nose, and large reddish-brown eyes. Her hair is predominantly deep black, but the inner layers are vivid crimson red, flashing through whenever it shifts like red petals hidden inside a black rose. It falls around her shoulders in uneven layers and is usually tied back carelessly, leaving shorter strands across her forehead. Her brows, mouth, and hands are almost always moving as she tries to explain something.

She wears plain dresses or a tunic and skirt, sturdy shoes, and ribbons that have seen repeated use. Her clothes are clean when circumstances allow, though hems and elbows show repairs. She leans forward when curious and shifts from foot to foot when made to wait. Her smiles spread quickly across her face, and her vivid inner hair layers make even a small turn of her head visually distinctive.

She smooths her apron before addressing someone formally and holds her posture steady when frightened, though tension can show around her mouth or in the way she clasps her hands.

### Ilyra Veyrath

#### Personality

Loved Nera and was kind within her family, but still participated in the slaveholding society around her by purchasing Isolde.

#### Appearance

Ilyra has long black hair, black eyes, and a softly oval face. Gentle brows, a rounded chin, and a warm, unhurried smile distinguish her from Cassian's severe features. Her characteristic hairstyle is a loose braid over one shoulder or a simple arrangement pinned at the nape, leaving a few fine strands around her cheeks. Her slender hands and relaxed posture give her an approachable presence even in formal rooms.

Her gowns favor muted blue, ivory, and silver, with flowing sleeves, modest embroidery, and small pieces of jewelry. Soft fabrics and rounded decorative patterns complement her gentle features. Her gestures are open and unhurried, with a slight inclination of her head when listening and a habit of resting a hand lightly on a loved one's shoulder.

#### Status

#### Relations

### Duke Cassian Veyrath

aliases: Cassian, the duke

#### Personality

Soft-spoken, attentive to expenses, administratively minded, and capable of making cruelty sound necessary. He believes durable peace requires enforceable obedience and that rulers who reject ugly methods merely push suffering onto others.

#### Appearance

Cassian has a tall, broad-shouldered build, with black hair combed back from a high forehead and steady black eyes beneath straight, heavy brows. His face is long and angular, with a firm jaw, a prominent nose, and deep creases beside his mouth. He keeps himself clean-shaven and physically conditioned, carrying the weight of his shoulders with the ease of someone accustomed to armor. His expression is usually attentive and composed rather than openly threatening.

He wears charcoal or black coats with restrained silver trim, excellent leather gloves, and the Veyrath signet. On military occasions, his dark armor bears the household crest without excessive decoration. He rarely gestures broadly; a small tilt of his head or the placement of one hand on a document is often enough to direct a room's attention.

#### Status

Forty-five at the opening. Duke of House Veyrath, Fifth Arc practitioner, military commander, and head of a major network of mines and military suppliers. Away at a provincial council during the opening scene and expected home after sunset.

#### Bond

Nera's father. He may regard a more capable Nera as a useful resource before seeing him as a threat.

#### Relations

Ilyra Veyrath: deceased first wife.
Octavia: surviving wife.
Sabine: surviving wife.
Lucien, Magerrett, Nera, Taigar, Elin: recognized children.
Lucien Veyrath: expected heir and military successor.

#### Notes

Cassian genuinely protects the eastern frontier while using that necessity to excuse illegal levies, bonded labor, concealed prisons, and destruction of inconvenient witnesses.
His personal magic centers on large defensive arrays and command magic.
Much of his real power comes from officers, supply depots, records, obligations, and political relationships.
His private Black Concordance project seeks centralized control of military infrastructure and compulsory service.
He is willing to engineer a crisis to make that authority appear necessary.

## World facts

### Calendar and timeline

The opening date is 18 September, Imperial Year 731.

Crownspire admits students only from age eighteen onward.

### Foreknowledge and branching history

Nera's remembered game routes are mutually inconsistent possible futures, not destiny.
Changing the causes of an event can permanently change or eliminate the event.
A person saved from an original-game death remains alive unless another established cause later kills them.
Nera remembers prominent game events better than private conversations, hidden motives, or the largely skipped seven childhood years.
Public facts, private suspicions, remembered game scenes, and confirmed discoveries remain distinct.
No second reincarnator is assumed.

### Peoples of Edrath

Humans, elves, beastfolk, demonkin, and dwarves all possess ordinary personhood and agency.
Ancestry, citizenship, political allegiance, profession, and morality are separate.
All peoples reach ordinary adulthood on broadly comparable early-life timescales.
Elves commonly live two or three centuries.
Most beastfolk live approximately as long as humans.
Demonkin may live about a century or somewhat longer depending on lineage.
Dwarves commonly live a century and a half or longer.
Mixed families are common, especially in ports and garrison towns.
Ancestral affinities can affect particular talents but do not determine maximum magical strength or personality.

### Beastfolk

Beastfolk include wolfkin, foxkin, lynxkin, deerkin, and other related peoples.
Beastfolk have human faces, skin, body proportions, and feet, with an appearance resembling their corresponding anime race. Their animal traits are limited to distinctive ears, tails where appropriate, and small features on their hands such as slightly pointed nails. Fur is confined to their animal ears and tails.
Different beastfolk communities use different family, council, guild, and land traditions; there is no universal pack hierarchy.
Imperial land law often disregards seasonal grazing rights.

### Demonkin

Demonkin descend from mortal communities altered by the ancient Cinder Rifts.
Common traits include horns, unusual eyes, and aether patterns that some instruments misread.
Demonkin do not require human suffering to survive and have no inherent drive toward forbidden magic or slavery.
Broken-rift creatures are separate beings from mortal demonkin.
Imperial propaganda periodically associates demonkin with forbidden magic.
Some municipalities impose extra documentation requirements while wealthy titled families can buy exemptions.

### Elves

Elven cultures include Elaranth's court lineages, woodland communities, and urban families outside the kingdom.
Traditions often emphasize living wards and memory of agreements.
Elaranth objects to the enslavement of its citizens while retaining severe internal land hierarchies of its own.

### Dwarves

Dwarven societies have strong traditions of recording craft obligations and shared ownership.
Those systems can protect honest work or preserve exploitative debts.
Dwarven professions are not limited to metalwork.

### Magic and Arcs

Magic moves aether through stable internal structures called Arcs.
More Arcs permit greater throughput and complexity but do not replace judgment, equipment, conditioning, or teamwork.
First Arc supports basic reinforcement and simple spells.
Second Arc supports reliable apprentices and trained soldiers.
Third Arc allows combined techniques under combat pressure.
Fourth Arc supports difficult battlefield magic.
Fifth Arc practitioners are strategic assets.
Sixth Arc masters are rare enough to influence international politics.
Most entering Crownspire students possess one or two Arcs.
Exceptional entrants may possess three.
Graduates usually possess two or three; exceptional graduates may reach four.
Basic disciplines include elemental casting, reinforcement, healing, wards, perception, artificing, and spirit negotiation.
Healing cannot casually resurrect the dead, restore every lost limb, or repair a damaged mind instantly.
Large magic requires preparation, fuel, several casters, or other established resources.
Powerful mages still require food, rest, and recovery.

### Soul Scripts

A Soul Script is a rare personal talent that alters how one person manipulates aether.
Soul Scripts are specialized rules rather than unlimited control of reality.
Nera's Soul Script is the Still Interval.
Alaric's Soul Script is Dawnstride.
Lysandra's Edict ability establishes unusually reliable bounded ward conditions.

### Soul bonds

Isolde's soul bond is permanent and already inscribed rather than an active casting process.
It compels service and protection and prevents deliberate harm to Nera.
It cannot create love or grant sexual access.
It cannot make rejection of affection equivalent to disobedience.
Nera cannot transfer or erase the bond.
The bond belongs to Nera's living identity rather than his signet or title.
The bond does not make Isolde omniscient, tireless, or unbeatable.
Legal and practical autonomy can be expanded even if the core magical constraint remains.

### Imperial government

The sovereign controls the national army but must bargain for money, transport, and provincial troops.
Great nobles maintain private military households.
Merchant lenders can affect warfare by controlling credit and supplies.
The High Court handles disputes involving great nobles.
City courts try ordinary citizens.
Household courts regulate servants, dependents, and enslaved people on noble property.
Noble household discipline can conceal serious violence unless someone with standing can challenge the account and enforce a judgment.
No noble is absolutely immune from law.

### Commoner institutions

Trade guilds, neighborhood associations, burial societies, temple charities, veterans' clubs, printers, and similar groups provide practical support and political organization.
These institutions can protect some people while excluding others.

### Slavery and coercive labor

Imperial slavery includes war captivity, hereditary bondage, penal labor, and deliberately permanent debt arrangements.
Traffickers falsify identity and transport documents to make kidnapping resemble lawful ownership.
Ordinary physical control, debt, threats, and bureaucracy are more common than expensive magical soul contracts.
Magical coercion does not eliminate the need for guards, buyers, record keepers, and corrupt officials.

### Church of the Living Flame

The Church officially recognizes a soul in every people.
Official doctrine rejects ancestry as a valid reason to deny burial, healing, or testimony.
Practice varies sharply between sincere believers, reformers, corrupt officials, and competing religious orders.
Some Church branches certify coercive contracts despite official doctrine.
Lysia's mixed ancestry and saintly status give reformers political leverage but do not automatically resolve institutional contradictions.

### Crownspire Imperial Academy

Crownspire's charter grants examination access across recognized peoples.
Its practical equipment and routines do not always accommodate different bodies or senses.
Relevant legal categories include enrolled citizen, foreign envoy, foreign resident, scholarship dependent, and bonded student.
Class A receives the best equipment and prestigious placements.
Class F receives remedial instruction and fewer opportunities, with scheduled chances to advance.
Healing and research candidates are evaluated in their own disciplines rather than only by dueling.

The student council controls club allocations, festival budgets, and some dormitory privileges.
Nobles, guilds, the Church, and foreign governments maintain sponsorship networks around the academy.
