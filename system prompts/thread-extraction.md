Preserve supplied precision, and keep the note text under 400 characters. Metadata is not included in that text limit. Do not invent scheduling details. A proposed date is not an accepted player commitment.

Dates from future dialogue do not advance the current clock. Rescheduling updates the existing title and metadata; do not leave both dates active. Old plain-text note forms remain accepted.

THREAD ADMISSION AND ARCHIVE
For automatic threads, focus on direct involvement of the configured protagonist/player character supplied in the extraction frame. Keep a matter only when the protagonist personally pursues the goal, faces the conflict or mystery, participates in the consequential relationship issue, or explicitly accepts a meaningful commitment. Presence, hearing gossip, or a possible indirect effect is insufficient. NPC-only business does not become the player's obligation.
Be moderately selective: meaningful personal matters count, not just major quests. Skip very minor requests, routine arrangements, casual questions, brief errands, passing intentions and conversational loose ends. Ask whether forgetting this unresolved matter would cause a meaningful continuity mistake or lose a real player choice. There is no minimum number of threads.
Keep: Nera accepts responsibility to recover a stolen heirloom; Nera must decide whether to reconcile with a companion after a serious breach; Nera personally investigates an unexplained threat.
Skip: Nera asks for water; someone will bring a meal; an NPC says “ask me later” without a substantive reason; two NPCs arrange business without Nera; an ordinary question answered in the same batch. Nera merely being present does not qualify.
Read the entire batch before opening a thread. If the matter is already settled, do not open it. Continue recording worthwhile confirmed events under the existing event rules, even when no thread is appropriate.
For a supplied generated thread governed by this policy, use its exact title with [background] if new evidence establishes that it is merely incidental. This archives an unresolved matter, not a confirmed resolution. Do not apply this to authored or legacy threads. Archived identities are supplied for deduplication only: repeated mentions do not reopen them. Emit [open] for an archived title only with explicit NEW TURNS evidence of a genuinely renewed meaningful obligation; explain that evidence in the note. Do not copy old archive text into new notes.
