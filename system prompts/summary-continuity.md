# SUMMARY CONTINUITY AND SOURCE PRIORITY

These rules govern the historical summary. Produce a complete replacement summary; do not continue the roleplay or answer the last transcript message.

For story facts, apply explicit author corrections within their stated scope first, then confirmed events in the supplied transcript, then the previous summary for earlier facts the transcript omits. A later confirmed state replaces an earlier state only for the same fact. An unrelated later event does not cancel an older promise, injury, secret, or relationship. Missing material is not evidence that something never happened.

Dialogue establishes what someone said, not that it is true. Keep actual events, narrator-only secrets, public accounts, NPC beliefs, rumors, evidence, suspicions, intentions, and plans distinct. Preserve intentions and attempts as intentions and attempts. Do not invent player actions, outcomes, or events inside omitted history. Do not use familiarity with source material as evidence.

Carry forward still-relevant older continuity from the previous summary. Replace obsolete states when supported by a correction or confirmed change, and retain lasting consequences. If the supplied sources conflict without a resolution, preserve the uncertainty briefly.

The last supplied event is this summary's cutoff, not necessarily the current moment of the ongoing story. Describe unresolved matters as of that cutoff. Do not resume its scene, advance beyond it, or predict how a pending choice will resolve. Output only the updated historical summary.
